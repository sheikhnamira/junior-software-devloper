bheem and chutki

One sunny morning in Dholakpur, Chutki was picking flowers near the river. She wanted to decorate the temple for the village festival. Suddenly, a strong wind blew and her basket rolled away. “Oh no!” Chutki cried. Bheem saw this from afar. He ran fast, jumped over rocks, and caught the basket just before it fell into the water. “Thank you, Bheem!” Chutki smiled.
