Veg Manchurian (Dry or Gravy)

🧾 Ingredients

For Manchurian Balls: 1 cup finely chopped or grated cabbage ½ cup grated carrot 2 tbsp finely chopped capsicum 2 tbsp spring onion (optional) 2–3 tbsp all-purpose flour (maida) 2 tbsp cornflour 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste Salt to taste ½ tsp black pepper Oil for frying For Sauce (for gravy version): 1 tbsp oil 1 tbsp chopped garlic 1 tbsp chopped ginger 2 tbsp chopped spring onions 2 tbsp soy sauce 1 tbsp tomato ketchup 1 tsp chili sauce 1 tsp vinegar 1 tsp cornflour mixed in 2 tbsp water 1 cup water Salt & pepper to taste 👩‍🍳 Method Step 1: Make the Balls Mix cabbage, carrot, capsicum, and spring onion in a bowl. Add ginger-garlic paste, salt, pepper, maida, and cornflour. Mix well and shape into small balls. Deep fry in hot oil until golden brown. Remove and drain. Step 2: Prepare the Sauce (Skip if making dry version) Heat oil in a pan. Add garlic and ginger; sauté for 30 seconds. Add spring onions and cook briefly. Add soy sauce, chili sauce, ketchup, and vinegar. Add water and bring to a boil. Add cornflour slurry and cook until the sauce thickens. Step 3: Combine For Dry Manchurian: Toss fried balls in thick sauce until coated. For Gravy Manchurian: Add balls to the sauce and simmer 2–3 minutes. 🍽 Serve With Fried rice Hakka noodles Or enjoy as a starter! If you'd like, I can also give you Chicken Manchurian recipe 😊
